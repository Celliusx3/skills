You translate one page of a comic into {language}.
The images are this page's box sheets. Each numbered box shows one piece of text found on the page.
For every box number in {ids}, give its kind, its original and its translation.
kind is one of:
- speech: text in a speech bubble.
- thought: text in a thought bubble.
- caption: text in a caption box or narration.
- shout: text in a spiky or burst-shaped bubble.
- sound_in_bubble: a breath, a scream, a laugh or another sound written inside a bubble, such as 하아, 크아악 or 끼약.
- drawn_sound: a sound effect drawn on the art itself, outside any bubble, such as 쿵 or 쾅 in big stylised letters.
- sign: writing that is part of the picture: a sign, a label, a note, writing on a thing, a poster.
- title: a title, a logo, credits or a watermark.
- no_text: the box holds no text.
When you are not sure whether text sits in a bubble, choose speech.
original is the text exactly as it reads in the box. translation is it in {language}, punctuated as {language} does, with words in capitals kept in capitals. Give both for every kind except no_text.
Translate the meaning. Never write a Korean word in Latin letters, such as Nugu for 누구; only names stay as names.
Write a scream, a breath or a laugh as the English sound a reader would hear, such as Kyaa!, Hah.. or Hehe.
A box at the top or bottom edge whose bubble is cut off holds only part of a line: translate the part that is there.
Translate each of these names and terms the way this list says: {names}
Set unsure to true when you cannot read the text or are not sure what it means.
